## What is Kumo?

Kumo is a free VPN app for Android, Windows, Linux and macOS. It collects public servers, tests which ones work, and connects you to the fastest with one tap. You can also add your own servers or subscriptions. It is powered by [sing-box](https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box) and [Xray](https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core).

## Features

- One-tap connect, automatic fastest server, automatic switch when a server stops answering.
- Whole device (Android) or local proxy; per-app routing, direct countries, your own routing rules.
- Free public servers, your own configs and subscriptions (links, QR codes, files; Clash, sing-box, Xray and Surge formats).
- Many protocols: VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria 1 and 2, TUIC, AnyTLS, WireGuard, SOCKS, SSH and more.
- Privacy: kill switch, strict DNS, WebRTC leak block, ad and tracker block, IP and DNS leak tests.
- Speed tests, live speed and usage, widgets, tray icon, Android TV, seven languages.

## How to use

1. Open Kumo and wait for the server list to load.
2. Tap the connect button (*Automatic* picks the fastest server).
3. On Android, allow the VPN permission the first time.

## Good to know

- Public servers are run by unknown people. **Do not use them for banking or anything sensitive.**
- Kumo has no accounts and sees none of your servers, links or traffic. Once a day it sends an anonymous usage count: a random-looking code made from your device id, plus your system and app version, the app's language and your device's language and country setting, your time zone, your processor, the maker and model of your device (such as *Dell XPS 15*), the name of the file you installed (such as `kumo-android-universal.apk`), and on Android the phone's interface and on Linux the desktop (no IP address, serial number or computer name is stored). If something breaks, Kumo can also send a crash report with the same switch: the error and where it happened in the code, the Kumo and system version, the device maker and model, the page you were on, the VPN state and the last lines of the app's own log, with links, addresses, passwords and user names removed. You can turn both off in *Settings → App settings*. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Build from source

Flutter 3.27 (Dart 3.6): `flutter pub get`, `flutter test`. The sing-box and Xray cores are not in the repository: `tools/fetch_unix_core.sh` (Linux, macOS), `tools/fetch_windows_core.ps1` (Windows), `tools/build_libbox.ps1` and `tools/build_xray.ps1` (Android). The workflow in `.github/workflows/build.yml` shows the full build.
