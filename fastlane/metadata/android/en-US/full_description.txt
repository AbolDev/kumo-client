Kumo is a free, open-source VPN client built on sing-box and Xray.

<b>What it does</b>
• Connects with one tap to the servers and subscriptions you add
• Many protocols: VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, TUIC, WireGuard, XHTTP and more
• Subscription links with usage and expiry, automatic updates, and the routing rules a provider sends
• Automatic mode picks the fastest servers and switches when one stops answering
• Per-app routing, country bypass lists, custom routing rules, kill switch
• Strong ad and tracker blocking, DNS leak test, what-is-my-IP check
• Quick Settings tile, home-screen widgets, Android TV support
• Interface in English, Persian, Russian, Vietnamese, Chinese and Arabic
