# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 9 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 8

- **The speed test has a new look** (*Tools > Speed test*). A big start button; the place, the way (through the VPN or not) and the most data (50 MB by default) are chips that open a sheet. While it runs, only the test is on the page: a ring in three parts (delay, download, upload) with the speed in the middle, drawn as it comes, four times a second, instead of only at the end of each part. Then the three results, *Share*, *Test again* and the last tests.
- **Find a clean IP shows how long it has left.** The steps (connect, real test, speed, data center) are on top with a tick for each one done, a bar of the whole scan, about how long is left and the time gone, and what it is doing now ("Measuring the speed of 104.18.217.48 through the server…"). The speed step no longer looks stuck at the end of the one before. Six real tests run at once (two in the gentle mode).
- **Find a clean IP: a slow upload gets its number.** The upload of an address sends the size you chose, in at most 20 seconds; when a slow line does not finish in that time, the speed shown is that of what it sent (0.6 Mbit/s shows as 0.6, not as nothing).
- **The usage count is sent a few seconds after the app opens** (3 seconds after the settings are read, instead of after the servers are loaded and the subscriptions updated). The switch in *Settings > App settings* is unchanged.
- **The privacy policy and the READMEs.** The policy says Kumo has no ad or bandwidth-sharing code. The README is now in all seven languages of the app.

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1) to beta 8. Most of it was checked by tests, not on every kind of device, so please tell us what you see.
