# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 8 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 7

- **A speed test** (*Tools > Speed test*). Download, upload and delay, through your VPN when one is connected and of your network itself when none is. Cloudflare by default; LibreSpeed (Frankfurt, Amsterdam, London) or OVH (download only) can be chosen. Each part stops at the amount of data you pick (20 to 500 MB, 100 by default). The last 30 tests are kept on your device to compare servers.
- **New Android widgets.** A power button (1×1), a quick bar (2×1) and a status card (2×2), smaller by default and resizable: wider, the bar shows the speed and the delay and the card becomes a full card with the time connected. They follow the phone between light and dark and take the wallpaper's colours on Android 12 and later; each can be set to light, dark or see-through instead. The widgets on your home screen change to the new look by themselves. The quick settings tile shows the server under its name.
- **Why a connection failed**, in a few words under the status (no answer in time, refused, name not found, and so on).
- **Anti-filter settings for a server** (*Edit config*, VLESS, VMess and Trojan): fingerprint (now with `unsafe`), ALPN, final mask (JSON) and cipher suites (`cipherSuites=` in the link). A server with any of the last three, or the unsafe fingerprint, runs on Xray by itself. Left empty, nothing changes.
- **Find a clean IP:** the delay sits under the address (which was torn in two), the count found and each speed show the moment they come, the speed stage lists the measured addresses first, and the most data of each speed test is yours to pick (2 to 32 MB).
- The page with no servers no longer flashes for a moment when the app opens with servers, and the server and traffic cards are hidden while there is no server.

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1) to beta 7. Most of it was checked by tests, not on every kind of device, so please tell us what you see.
