# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 4 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 3

- **A stronger ad blocker** (*Settings > Network > Block ads and trackers*). A third level, *Maximum*, merges the biggest public lists (HaGeZi Ultimate, OISD Big, the AdGuard DNS filter, PersianBlocker, and HaGeZi's lists of pop-up ads and of the trackers of phone and TV makers) into one; it uses more memory, and now and then a site needs an exception. The lists are downloaded again once after the update and then every day, from more mirrors. A list of about 100,000 names is built into the app, so ads are blocked from the first connection even when the lists cannot be downloaded. Settings shows how many lists and names are in use and when they were updated, with *Update now*.
- **Never block.** A list of the sites and apps that ad blocking should leave alone, for when one stops working. Names that apps need are never blocked, even when a list has them: the Facebook and Instagram API, YouTube, the players of video sites, app links, newsletter links, support chat.
- **Cookie banners and affiliate links.** Two optional switches under ad blocking: one hides most cookie-consent banners, the other blocks the redirects of affiliate links (a "buy at ..." link that goes through one does not open).
- **No way around the blocker through encrypted DNS.** At the strong and maximum levels, apps can no longer reach a public encrypted DNS server (Google, Cloudflare, Quad9, AdGuard and others) by its address, so their lookups go through the blocker.
- **Find a clean IP** is now also in the Home menu, has more options, and you can tick the addresses you want and add them to one or more of your configs, as copies in a group of their own (*Clean IPs*).

Ad blocking works by names. Ads that come from the same server as the content (YouTube's own video ads, Instagram's sponsored posts) cannot be blocked this way. With an OpenVPN server on Android the ad blocker does not apply.

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1), beta 2 and beta 3. Most of it was checked by tests, not on every kind of device, so please tell us what you see.
