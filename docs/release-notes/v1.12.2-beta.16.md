# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 16 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 15

- **Android widgets, much more to choose.** The look page of a widget is now in tabs (looks, colours, card, button, text, shown) and shows the real widget on your wallpaper, in the state and at the size you pick (1×1 to 4×2) to see how the look follows the size.
  - *Looks:* seven ready-made ones (Default, Minimal, Glass, Bold, Night, Sharp, Floating); looks of your own that you save with a name and put on other widgets, or on all of them at once; a button back to the default.
  - *Colours:* follow the phone, light or dark, the opacity, an accent (blue, purple, orange, red, green, the wallpaper's, or any colour you compose with the picker), a card colour of your own, the colour of the connected button (green or the accent) and a button colour for each state.
  - *Card:* the roundness of the corners, the height of the card, a border (1 to 3 dp, in the accent or a neutral colour), a depth (flat, raised, floating) and the spacing inside.
  - *Button:* its shape (circle, rounded square, square), five icons, its size (Android 12 and later) and the side it stands on.
  - *Text:* the size of the texts, bold or regular, the typeface, the alignment, a title of your own instead of the server's name, your own words for each state, and the language of the widget, apart from the language of the app.
  - *Shown:* each of the server, the speeds, the delay, the data used and the session time can be left out; an extra line about the server (its protocol, or the flag in its name); bytes or bits for the speeds; and what a tap does (open the app, or connect and disconnect).
  - The delay now stands in the row of the live speeds of the quick bar. The power button is green while connected. A widget with big texts or a big button gives the room back when it has none. The list of widgets shows the connected look with example numbers; on Android 15 and later it draws the real widget.
  - The settings are in all six languages of the app. **The widgets have not been tried on a phone yet:** if one looks wrong or does not draw, tell us the Android version and the launcher.
- **Logs as a file.** *Settings > Logs* can save the lines shown as a file, or share it from the phone.
- **Starting again from the tile or the widget works after the lists were cleaned.** The cleaning of the lists no longer deletes a file that a saved connection names, so a connection started from the tile, the widget or at boot did not find the subscription's ad list missing.
- **WARP sign-up.** The progress bar cannot come back after the run ended, and the MASQUE account is not asked for a second time while the bar stands still.
- **Windows installer.** An update says it is one (titles and button, no license page), the update made by the program is silent and Kumo starts again at its end, a running Kumo is told to disconnect before it is closed, the uninstall asks whether to delete settings and servers and removes the OpenVPN adapter, and the wizard is in English, Persian, Russian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic. The Visual C++ runtime is shipped next to `kumo.exe` in the x64 and ARM64 builds. **The installer has not been tried on a real update yet.**

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1) to beta 15. The WARP server that fails when connected (*wg-noise-a*, *wg-noise-b*) is not fixed yet: if you see it, send the log (*Settings > Logs*) after a few minutes of connection.
