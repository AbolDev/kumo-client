# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 13 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 12

- **WARP over MASQUE.** Besides WireGuard, WARP can now connect over MASQUE, Cloudflare's own way, on HTTP/2 (TCP) or HTTP/3 (UDP), each also with another server name (it runs on Xray). It often passes where WireGuard is cut. A new WARP account starts on MASQUE over HTTP/2, and an account made before moves to it once; a way you pick by hand afterwards stays. The MASQUE ways over HTTP/2 can take a TLS fingerprint of their own (*WARP page > TLS fingerprint*).
- **Every way of WARP is a server you can test.** The WARP group on the main screen has one server per method (*WARP wg-plain*, *WARP masque-h2*, ...). Test them with the TCP ping or the real delay of the group's menu, and pick one like any server; a test never takes a WARP server out. The WARP page is now in cards (ways to connect, more options, data used, profile), without the list of methods and without *Try another way*; the method, address and port are no longer written on the pages.
- **A faster WARP sign-up, with a progress bar.** Making the account tried the ways to Cloudflare one after the other, which could take minutes. Now the ways are tried at the same time and the first that gets through is used, the WireGuard and MASQUE accounts are made together, and the hello is cut in pieces that pass more networks. A bar with the percent shows on the WARP page and on its card in *Free connections*. When MASQUE cannot be made, WARP starts on WireGuard and MASQUE is tried again at the next start.
- **Warp-in-Warp over MASQUE and noise.** Warp-in-Warp took plain WireGuard only. Now the outer tunnel, the one the filter sees, can be any way of the group (MASQUE, WireGuard with noise or plain), with WARP's own tunnel inside it, so the group has a *WARP in WARP* server for each method. Sites may see an exit that is not in your country.
- **WARP through another server.** A row on the WARP page sends WARP through one of your servers (the same as *Proxy chain* in the network options), so Cloudflare sees that server's address instead of yours and the exit is more likely not an Iranian one. The MASQUE ways do not take it.
- **Sleep timer and daily data cap** (*Settings > App settings*). The connection ends a set time after it is made, or when today's data reaches the cap; the reason is shown. On the phone they work with the screen off.
- **Shortcuts and a command line.** On Android, press and hold Kumo's icon for *Connect* and *Disconnect* (not shown while a disguise is on). On a computer, `kumo --connect`, `--disconnect` and `--toggle` work on the running copy, with no window; a copy that is not running starts and connects.
- **Widgets that fit their size.** The quick bar, the status card and the power button change their layout with their width and height, down to one cell or one row, always left to right, with numbers in English and smaller corners. Their settings show the real widget at its size on your own wallpaper, the colour (follow the phone, light, dark) and the opacity from 0 to 100%, with *Cancel* and *Save*. The widget has one colour in both states; only the power button changes (outlined when off, filled when on), and below half the opacity the texts are white so they read on the wallpaper.
- **The speed test shows the delay through Xray.** On the phone the delay was always empty through Xray servers (WARP over MASQUE among them); it is measured now. The real delay through Xray also works when the delay address you chose is https.
- **Fixes from the crash reports.** Xray servers that asked to skip the certificate check (Xray 26 no longer takes it), servers with a pinned certificate, Shadowsocks servers with the cipher name `chacha20-poly1305`, OpenVPN on Windows with OpenVPN 2.7, a tunnel on Android when the phone gives no IPv6 address, a VPN start on Android that the system closed, and Xray's port when it was still held. On Windows the core is started once more when the tunnel adapter of the last connection was not gone yet. Network errors are no longer sent as crash reports, the automatic mode no longer falls back to a server with a refused Reality key or a dangerous one, and *Cancel update* and *Cancel test* in a group's menu follow the state while the menu is open.

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1) to beta 12. WARP over MASQUE, the WARP group, the widgets and the speed test were tried on one Android phone; Warp-in-Warp over MASQUE and noise, the new sign-up and the Windows retry were checked by tests only, so please tell us what you see.
