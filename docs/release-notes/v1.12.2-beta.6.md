# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 6 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 5

- **A new look for the home page.** The three-dot menu holds *Tools* (a page with a line about each tool) and *Restart*. The actions on all servers (ping all, real delay, clear results, update subscriptions) are above the list, which shows how far a test is, with *Cancel*; each group shows its own progress too. Swipe a server to the end to delete it (with *Undo*) or to the start to share it; press and hold to pick several (test, copy links, save to my configs, delete). With no server there are three big buttons: paste, scan a code, free connections. The last page of the guide adds a first server. Deleting a server, a subscription or all servers of a group can be undone.
- **Settings.** Network is split into *Network*, *Routing*, *DNS*, *Ad blocker* and *Advanced*, each with *Reset this section*. A long description is cut to two lines under a switch, with the rest behind an information button; advanced options and the kill switch carry a label. *Colors*: Kumo's teal (as before) or the system's (Android 12 and later, Windows accent); *Pure black in dark mode*; *Vibration feedback* when you connect or disconnect (phone).
- **Persian and Arabic are shown in the Vazirmatn font.**
- **Logs.** Search, *Only problems*, and *Copy for a bug report* (with the version and the system; links and addresses are removed).
- **Find a clean IP: speed.** With a server, the best addresses can be measured for download and/or upload speed through that server, and sorted by it. Results of a scan with a server now show as the addresses are found, not only after the real test. Up to 10,000 addresses are picked with buttons.
- **The ad lists are fetched through the connection** where the app's own traffic would go out the network's own way (a computer in the proxy mode, a phone with Xray). *Settings > Network > Routing > Download the lists* chooses: through the connection when it is up (the default), only through it, or directly. *Block public encrypted DNS* has a switch for a phone whose Private DNS names such a server.
- **Smaller and lighter.** The built-in ad list is a binary file (0.8 MB, was 2 MB), the Windows ARM64 installer is compressed as the x64 one, old list files are cleaned up, the max list is merged without holding every list in memory, saved pings take half the space, and a second copy's links are noticed without looking every 2 seconds.
- **Fixes.** The reason of a disconnect is noted on the desktop too; the saving of a stopped scan ends with it; at most 64 probes at once in a scan, with a hint for gentle mode on big ones; the screen reader finds the settings search; the *Back* button leaves the results at once when the scanner was opened from a config.

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1) to beta 5. Most of it was checked by tests, not on every kind of device, so please tell us what you see.
