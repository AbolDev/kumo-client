# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 14 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 13

- **Making a WARP account works on networks in Iran again.** The service that makes the account cuts the connection when its name is in one piece of the TLS hello, and when the pieces are too small. On an Iranian network, pieces of 40 to 80 bytes got through every time, while the pieces the profile asked for (5 to 40 bytes) never did, and the account took 24 seconds to fail. Now the cuts that pass are tried first, all at once, and a call to the service whose connection failed is tried again on a new connection. From the same network the WireGuard account takes about 3 seconds and the MASQUE one about 4.
- **Protocols.** HTTP and HTTPS proxies (`http://user:pass@host:port`, and `https://` with `sni` and `insecure`) are servers now. A pasted address is taken for a proxy only when it has the port written out, no path, a login or an IP address; the address of a subscription, which has a path or a query of its own, is never taken for one, and a proxy is never fetched as a subscription. Clash lists keep their HTTP, SSH and WireGuard servers and the plugin of a Shadowsocks server (obfs, v2ray-plugin, ShadowTLS); sing-box lists keep HTTP and NaiveProxy outbounds, WireGuard endpoints, plugins, and a Shadowsocks server behind a ShadowTLS outbound. A server with a plugin Kumo lacks is left out instead of being added broken.
- **The update notice comes sooner.** The check for a newer stable release starts with the app instead of after the start and three more seconds, and the answer of the last check is kept for three days, so a newer version already known is offered the moment the app is ready.
- **Switches and buttons in settings.** The switch of an option stands at the end of its title and the description takes the whole width under it, in every page with switches. Buttons that were at the end of a row (*Cancel* of a scan, *Update now* of the ad lists, and the buttons of the clean IP page) are under their text. The WARP page has a line between the options.
- **The scans of the WARP page show what they do.** While one runs: a bar, how many ways were tried and answered, about how long is left, the fastest so far, the last four ways tried with their delay or "no answer", and *Cancel*. When it ends, the ways that answered, fastest first, with the one in use marked. Their descriptions say how many ways are tried and how long it takes.
- **The speed test fills in more.** A download or upload cut short by its time limit gives the speed of what came, a stage whose connections all failed is tried once more on one connection, the upload is made even when its first small try failed, and the log says why (the answer of the server, no data, a timeout). Through Xray servers the delay is measured (it was always empty).
- **A new request for a star** with Kumo's cloud and a gold star, one big button and the other two answers side by side.
- **Telegram.** *Settings > About* has a row for Kumo's Telegram channel, and the READMEs link to it.
- **Less noise.** The "N works" line under the public servers and WARP is gone after a test (the bar shows the counts while it runs and the servers show their own delays afterwards).

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1) to beta 13. The WARP sign-up was tried on a real network in Iran (the account in both ways, and the old version for comparison); the rest was checked by tests, not on every kind of device, so please tell us what you see, above all the switches of the settings on Android TV.
