# Kumo 1.12.2 beta 11 (test version)

This is a test version (pre-release). It is not offered as an update unless *Include pre-releases* is on in Settings.

## What's new since beta 10

- **Android TV.** On a television Kumo has two panes (the connect card and the servers), the remote starts on the power button, and what has the focus is drawn with a clear ring. *Add from your phone* replaces the camera and the file picker: the screen shows a code, the phone opens its address, sends a link or a subscription, and the television adds it (asking first, as any adding does).
- **Sync devices** (*Settings > Backup*). Move your servers, clean IPs and subscriptions (and, if you want, how the app looks and behaves) to another device of yours over the local network, with no account and no server. One device shows a QR code (or its text) and the other scans it; the data is sealed with a key that only the code holds, and the code works once, for five minutes, on the local network only. What comes is never applied without a question that says what it holds; by default it is only added to what you have.
- **Disguise and erase** (*Settings > Disguise and erase*, Android). Show Kumo in the launcher as a calculator, notes, a clock, the weather, a calendar or files; or type any name and take the icon of any app on the phone (for a shortcut on the home screen and for the card in the recent apps); or hide the icon (open Kumo from a shortcut or by dialling `*#*#5866#*#*`). *Erase all data* deletes everything Kumo keeps and keeps the disguise; it asks, and then the button must be held for three seconds. Erasing by dialling `*#*#7263#*#*` is off until you turn it on.
- **Safety check of a server** (*Server menu > Safety check*). Well-known sites are fetched through the server and compared with what your network gets, to find a server that puts its own certificate in front of a secure site, changes a plain page, or does not hide your address. A server found changing traffic is left out of the automatic mode and asks before it is picked. It cannot find a server that only watches: whoever runs a server sees which sites you ask for, whatever the result.
- **The automatic mode connects faster.** Just before it connects, the servers of the group are tried with a plain TCP connect, and the ones that answer are put first, so a server that died since the last ping no longer costs a long wait at the first connection.
- **A better page for the routing rules.** Rules are added by choosing apps, popular services, websites, addresses and ports from lists; the page shows the three lists (block, through the VPN, direct) in the order they are applied, with colours, counts, a search, a test of what happens to a name, undo, moving a rule to another list, and import and export.
- **Tools redone.** *Service test* works with the VPN off and shows live results; *DNS leak test* shows the servers as they answer; *Why can't I connect* is clearer; *Share Kumo* keeps its language buttons in place and has a QR code with Kumo's logo. *Find the best settings* has a page of its own.
- **No JSON to write for the extra options.** The keep-alive and UDP options of sing-box and the socket options of Xray are a sheet of fields (JSON stays as an advanced way), and splitting the TLS hello of a server is a button.
- **WireGuard servers with no IPv6 address.** Apps ask for IPv6 first, and the tunnel could not carry it, so pages hung; now such a server is not given IPv6 (its allowed networks are IPv4 and names have no IPv6 answer). Only for a server picked on its own.
- **The icons of the apps in per-app routing** show again (a call to the system was answered by the wrong handler).
- **The progress of a list test** is part of the tile of the group, so it no longer cuts into the usage chips.

Everything else is as in [beta 1](https://github.com/AbolDev/kumo-client/releases/tag/v1.12.2-beta.1) to beta 10. Most of it was checked by tests, not on every kind of device (the television, the disguise and the erase, the sync and the safety check have not been tried on a real device yet), so please tell us what you see.
